Inside the pump room: a long read on harbour fuel

By Mira Okonkwo

The pump room under the east quay smells of diesel and seaweed, and for forty years nobody questioned the hourly rattle of the timers. This is the public preview of a longer piece about who finally did, and why it took a volunteer with a notebook rather than a contractor with a budget.

The preview continues with the first morning of readings, when the volunteer group found the pumps had run dry for most of the night and decided to write every figure down by hand.
