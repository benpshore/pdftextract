The long tide

Page one opens on the morning the harbour master counted the fuel drums for the first time in a decade. There were fewer than the ledger said, and the difference was not theft but the quiet appetite of twelve timers that nobody had reset since the pontoons were rebuilt.

The ledger

The ledger itself was a school exercise book, kept in the pump room, with a column for each delivery and a column for each reading that nobody had filled in since the previous winter. The volunteer group adopted it the same week.
