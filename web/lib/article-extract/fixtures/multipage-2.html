The long tide

Page two follows the group through the first storm week, when the sensors were new and the pumps were still on timers on half of the pontoons, so the two systems could be compared side by side through the worst weather of the season.

Side by side

The timers ran the pumps for the same ten minutes an hour whatever the weather did, while the sensor pontoons pumped only when the bilges filled, which during the storm was often and between storms was almost never.
