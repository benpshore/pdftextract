Architecture decision: keep Rust/lopdf for digital PDFs and the existing worker
boundary. Apple Vision is a platform-specific OCR adapter, not a PDF engine
migration. First correct extraction/status and safe planning; then expose local
attachment/format adapters under the same bounded, source-preserving boundary.
Do not import every open prototype to claim integration.

Apple's supported recognition API is
[VNRecognizeTextRequest](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vnrecognizetextrequest).
PhotoKit supplies images through
[requestImageDataAndOrientation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photos/phimagemanager/requestimagedataandorientation(for:options:resulthandler:));
network acquisition is controlled by
[isNetworkAccessAllowed](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photos/phimagerequestoptions/isnetworkaccessallowed).
Dry-run must not request authorization, open Photos/Zotero, invoke OCR, or call APIs.
Real Photos permission, iCloud-only assets, and Zotero integration cannot be
qualified by mocks or by a GitHub runner with no user's library.

## Correction and measured outcomes

Font diagnosis was reproduced by inspecting the pinned font dictionaries and
real output. Widthless standard Latin fonts previously advanced every glyph by
500 units, corrupting both spacing and column geometry. The new bounded lookup
uses standard Times/Helvetica metrics and Courier advances, honoring the rendered
encoding and explicit PDF widths. Metrics are pinned and Apache-licensed from
PDF.js; no PDF.js runtime, external engine, or dependency is added. Unembedded
Arial/Times aliases remain visibly uncertain; unknown/embedded widthless fonts
are not silently certified. Per-font cache accounting includes recovery storage.

The other order failures involved narrow three-column gutters, short tails in
fused rows and tight spanning headings masking the body column cut. Repeated
column evidence now supports these cases, with a shared finite work budget and
at most two split passes. Exhausted work and repeated unresolved side-by-side
prose become Partial, retaining the text and positional evidence.
